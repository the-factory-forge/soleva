# Copywriting

Read this guide before writing or revising public page copy, UI messages,
translations or metadata.

The shared contracts live in forge-spec's
[website creation spec](https://github.com/the-factory-forge/forge-spec/blob/main/openspec/specs/website-creation/spec.md)
and [site template spec](https://github.com/the-factory-forge/forge-spec/blob/main/openspec/specs/site-template/spec.md).
This guide applies those contracts alongside the template's writing conventions.
Keep customer-specific decisions in the customer's records.

## Start with the customer

- Read [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md), [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) and the existing
  customer brief. Identify the audience, actual offer, primary visitor action,
  confirmed facts and chosen tone before drafting.
- Preserve the customer's established voice across pages and languages. The
  starter's wording and The Corner Factory's identity are not customer defaults.
- Record missing facts and tone decisions in existing project documentation.
  Continue with supported content; omit unsupported claims or mark publication
  readiness incomplete when required inputs are missing.

## Write useful, factual content

- Answer the visitor's question directly. Service pages explain the actual offer;
  about and contact pages use confirmed identity and contact details; FAQs answer
  useful customer questions.
- Use confirmed evidence for prices, availability, credentials, testimonials,
  response times and service areas. Never invent claims to fill a section or
  satisfy a keyword target. Demo content is not evidence.
- Choose sections and their order around the visitor's needs and next action.
  Remove sections that have no useful customer content.
- When describing Forge itself, follow the
  [Forge positioning and offer qualifiers](https://github.com/the-factory-forge/forge-spec/blob/main/openspec/specs/the-forge/spec.md#requirement-preserve-forge-positioning-and-offer-qualifiers).
  Confirm current scope, prices and delivery commitments for the engagement.

## Use plain language

- Prefer familiar words, active verbs and short sentences. State what the customer
  offers or what the visitor can do. Replace vague praise with a supported detail.
- Keep headings descriptive and paragraphs focused on one idea. Use consistent
  terms for the same service or action.
- Label calls to action with the action they perform, such as "Request a quote"
  for an enquiry form. Use "Book an appointment" only when booking is available.
- For UI messages, explain the outcome and any next step. Mention providers or
  implementation details only when the user needs them to act. Error messages
  should explain how to recover when a recovery action exists.
- Apply the Unslop skill supplied by Harness when editing. Remove filler, inflated
  claims and formulaic phrasing while preserving meaning and the customer's tone.

## Keep search content and translations accurate

- Before materially changing SEO content, record visitor questions, relevant
  search topics and confirmed service areas in the existing brief or keyword
  document. Keep the source, date and uncertainty of research-derived keywords
  and search-volume claims. Distinguish proposed topics from measured demand.
- Use search terms naturally, with descriptive headings and relevant internal
  links or contact actions. There are no fixed keyword or FAQ quotas. Never
  promise rankings, rich results or inclusion in generative answers.
- Write a descriptive localized title and a page-specific description. Avoid
  repeated brand suffixes; summarize the page instead of repeating its title.
  Exact character counts are not universal acceptance criteria.
- Describe informative images in alternative text; use empty alternative text
  for decorative images.
- Review copy in every configured locale, including navigation and contact
  actions. Preserve confirmed meaning and tone. A translated website does not
  establish that the customer provides services in that language.
- Keep visible copy, metadata, structured data and discovery output consistent
  with the same confirmed customer facts. Customer identity stays distinct from
  the agency credit defined in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#essentials).

## Review before delivery

Read the draft for clarity, factual support, consistent terminology and a usable
next action. Replace or remove sample identities, placeholder text and unconfirmed
contact, social or booking links before declaring publication readiness.

For implemented copy changes, inspect the affected rendered pages and configured
locales. Check link destinations and corresponding metadata or discovery output
when affected. Record missing inputs and checks that could not be performed;
source edits alone do not prove the published result.
